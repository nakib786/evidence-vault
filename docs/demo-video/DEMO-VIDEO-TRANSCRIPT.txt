[00:00:00] Someone shouts abuse on a bus. Someone posts a threat about a mosque. By the time anyone reports it, the evidence is gone.

[00:00:38] Most of what this documents is spoken, not written — so the main way in is video, with sound.

[00:00:47] It's fingerprinted the instant you stop recording — before it's ever a file that could be edited. That fingerprint is a SHA-256 hash: change one frame and it changes completely.

[00:01:13] The recording stays blurred until they choose to look, and every field describing what happened is optional.

[00:02:08] The export is five files: the report, the original recording, the proof, the certificate, and a plain-text cover letter.

[00:02:24] Every capture lands in an on-device vault — encrypted with a PIN, never uploaded anywhere.

[00:02:45] Any past record can be re-downloaded — report, recording, proof and certificate — any time, without ever re-uploading anything to get it back.

[00:02:57] The person using this decides who else ever sees it. Nothing here depends on this app, or this device, still existing tomorrow.
